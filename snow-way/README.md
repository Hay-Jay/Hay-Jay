# Snow Way

Per-visit snow clearing in Regina, run by Deji and Jide as co-owners: a two-person student crew working around classes. There are no monthly or winter plans. The goal is to be the cheapest crew in the area.

Local competitors charge $30 to $40 for a single and $50 to $55 for a double. No GST until you earn over $30k a year.

## Service area and customers

- Start in your own neighbourhood, within about a 10-minute walk, on the 2 to 3 nearest streets.
- Best customers: seniors, people with disabilities, busy parents, shift workers, landlords and snowbirds.
- Selling point: the City makes owners clear sidewalks within 48 hours after snow stops, or it bills them.

## Equipment

- **Must-have kit, about $150 to $220:** 2 snow pushers, 1 scoop shovel, an ice chopper, a broom, 2 headlamps, 2 pairs of ice cleats and spare gloves. It pays for itself in about 7 jobs.
- **Later, from profits:** a used gas snowblower ($150 to $400) once you have 8+ customers, or a cordless one ($500 to $1,000). Also a sled, hi-vis vests and ice melt. Skip corded blowers.
- **Timing:** about 25 to 30 minutes per single driveway by hand in normal snow, or 12 to 15 minutes with a blower. That fits about 4 jobs before class and 8 to 10 on a snow day, roughly $250 to $300.
- **Realistic first season:** $1,500 to $4,000.

## Getting customers (under $10)

- Free posts on Facebook groups, Marketplace, Kijiji and Nextdoor. Re-post the night before snow.
- 50 half-page flyers, about $5, in mailboxes but not Canada Post community boxes.
- Knock on doors the morning after snow.
- Referrals: $10 off for each one that books.
- Clear a corner lot for free and leave a note.

## Running it

- Cap it at 10 to 12 houses and promise "same day or next morning." Waitlist anyone past that.
- Keep a shared Google Calendar of both class timetables. Check WeatherCAN each evening and text customers before snow.
- Plan routes street by street. Either of you can do light jobs alone.
- No work during blizzard warnings. Text honest ETAs and do seniors first.
- **Payment:** Interac e-Transfer with Autodeposit. Text a photo of the finished job and get paid the same day. Refuse overpayments, which are a common scam.
- **Partnership:** agree in writing to put 20% into an equipment fund and split the rest 50/50. Track jobs and payments in a shared Google Sheet.

## Legal and insurance

- Home insurance usually doesn't cover this work. Get a liability quote once you're earning steadily.
- Until then, never promise ice-free surfaces, photograph every job, keep tools away from cars, and never throw snow onto the road.
- Report all earnings as income.
