    Regina sidewalk rule

    Clear within 48 hours

    The City of Regina expects you to clear the sidewalk next to your property within 48 hours after snow stops. If it isn't done, the City can clear it and bill you. Book us and it's handled.

    The city plow also leaves a windrow, the heavy ridge across the end of your driveway. We clear that too for $10.

    Questions

    Things neighbours ask

    Something else? Text us and Deji or Jide will answer.

    How fast can you come?

    Same day or the next morning. We keep our list to about 12 houses so we can keep that promise around classes. If we're full, we'll tell you and put you on the waitlist. The crew status at the top of the page shows whether we're taking jobs today.

    Do I need to sign up for a plan?

    No. Every clear is a one-time visit. Book when you need us, and skip it when you don't.

    What's included in the price?

    Your driveway, the walk to your front door and the city sidewalk in front of your house. Steps, a back walk or deck, brushing off your car and ice melt are $5 each. Corner lots are $10 more for the extra sidewalk.

    What if there's a lot of snow?

    The price covers up to about 15 cm. Over 15 cm, or when the city plow leaves a ridge across your driveway, it's $10 to $15 more. We tell you before we start, and you can always say no.

    Do you deal with ice?

    We clear the snow and spread ice melt if you add it. Regina ice comes back fast, so we can't promise a walk with no ice on it.

    How do I pay?

    Interac e-Transfer or cash, after the job is done. We text you a photo first. We don't charge GST.

    Can I get you to come back every time it snows?

    Book once, then tap "Clear me again" on the My requests page each time. Your address and notes are saved. We also text our regulars the night before a big snow.

    What if you damage something?

    Put anything fragile in your notes, like garden beds or a low fence. If we damage something, we fix it or pay for it.
