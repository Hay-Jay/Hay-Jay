Regina sidewalk rule

Clear within 48 hours

The City of Regina expects you to clear the sidewalk in front of your home within 48 hours of a snowfall. Book us and it's handled.

The city plow also leaves a windrow, the heavy ridge across the end of your driveway. We clear that too for a few dollars.

Questions

Things neighbours ask

How fast can you come?

When you book, we text you an arrival time. We're students, so we plan around classes and tell you honestly when we can make it. Check the crew status at the top of the page to see if we're taking jobs today.

Do I need to sign up for a plan?

No. Every clear is a one-time visit. Book when you need us, and skip it when you don't.

What's included in the price?

Your driveway, the walk to your front door, the front steps, and the city sidewalk in front of your house. Windrows, ice melt, a back walk or deck, and brushing off your car are small add-ons.

What if there's a lot of snow?

The price covers a normal Regina snowfall. If there's more than about 25 cm or it's packed down hard, we'll tell you the price before we start. You can always say no.

How do I pay?

Interac e-Transfer or cash, after the job is done. We don't charge GST.

Can I get you to come back every time it snows?

Book once, then use "Clear me again" on the My requests page each time. Your address and notes are saved, so it takes one tap.

What if you damage something?

Put anything fragile in your notes, like garden beds or a low fence. If we damage something, we fix it or pay for it.
