Your Regina business, on a billboard in Regina Life

Regina Life is a free, cozy life game set in a bright, toy-sized Regina, with real Regina time and weather. Its billboards rotate ads from real local businesses, each clearly labelled Sponsored and linked to your website.

Book a billboard

Type your ad and watch it on a board in the game, then book it and pay right here with PayPal or a card. A person reviews every ad before it goes live; if we can't run yours, you get a full refund.

Colours

Prices

Prices are in Canadian dollars for 7 days. Each board shares its screen time with a few other ads, so every ad gets a fair turn. Bookings are reviewed by a person before they go live.

How it works

  1. Book it above. Pick a board, write your line, add your link and pay right here with PayPal or a debit or credit card. Your ad details travel with your payment.
  2. We review it against the ad rules below, usually within two days. If we can't run it, we refund you in full.
  3. It goes live for 7 days on your boards, labelled Sponsored with your website's name. Players who tap it see a "you're leaving the game" screen first.

Straight talk

  • We're new. We don't publish audience numbers yet, and we won't make them up. Founding rates reflect that.
  • Your money never touches the game's money. Prairie Dollars are fictional and can't be bought with your ad spend, and ads never change how the game plays.
  • No ad trackers. The game has no ad tracking, and your ad's link is a plain link to your site.

Ad rules

  • Real, legal Canadian businesses and community groups only.
  • No political or election ads, no alcohol, cannabis, tobacco, vaping, gambling or adult content, and nothing aimed at kids.
  • Your own words and your own brand: no other company's marks, no misleading claims (we follow the spirit of Ad Standards Canada's code).
  • Links must be https and go to your own site.
  • We may decline any ad. If we do, you get a full refund.

Love the game? Support it

Regina Life is free, and it will stay free. If you'd like to help keep it growing, you can support it directly. Supporters who want to can have their name painted on the Supporters' Mural in the game's Victoria Park. It's a thank-you, not a perk: support never buys anything in the game.
