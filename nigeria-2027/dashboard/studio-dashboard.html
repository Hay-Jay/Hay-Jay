Best quality from your iPhone

  1. Save the MP4 here (⬇ Download), then in the share sheet tap Save Video. It lands in Photos at full 1080×1920.
  2. TikTok: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Data saver: off. On the post screen, check More options → Allow high-quality uploads if your app shows it.
  3. Instagram: Settings → Media quality → Upload at highest quality: on, and Data saver off.
  4. Upload on Wi-Fi. Don't add TikTok filters or re-trim: re-editing re-compresses the video.
  5. Cover: on the post screen tap Edit cover → pick from your photos and choose the saved cover PNG.
  6. Paste the caption from Copy caption. Add 3–5 hashtags, not 20.
