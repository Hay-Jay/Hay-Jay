Platforms

Platform playbook

TikTok
  • Post 7–9pm WAT; Sunday news at 6pm.
  • Add a trending sound at 15–20% under the narrator.
  • Pin the Fact-check and Promise vs reality videos.
  • Reply to comments with video replies; it pushes reach.
Instagram Reels
  • Reels up to 3 min, so every video fits.
  • Post the stills as a carousel 1–2 days later.
  • Use Stories polls for the weekly viewer poll.
  • Put the website link in your bio.
X
  • Yes, use it: X is where Nigerian political debate happens.
  • Upload the video natively; free accounts allow 2:20, so all fit.
  • Lead with the strongest number in the first line.
  • Quote-post news the day it breaks; link sources in a reply.
Rules that protect you
  • Keep the sources card at the end of every video.
  • Say "alleged" until a court convicts.
  • Don't post unverified viral claims, even ones you like.
  • Check dates and numbers again on posting day.
