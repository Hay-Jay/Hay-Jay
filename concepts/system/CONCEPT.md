# SYSTEM — MONOCHROME OS v1.0

**Idea:** the site is the interface of a classified catalogue terminal from 2003, built to archive clothes from the future. It boots (`MONOCHROME OS v1.0 — loading archive… 23 objects`), then every product is a datasheet record (ID `MC-017`, category code, size key-caps, status LED) instead of a shop tile.
**What makes it distinct:** Martian Mono (variable width) against Big Shoulders Display with a chrome-gradient `UNKNOWN`; hairline grids, corner brackets, a left scroll ruler and top/bottom HUD bars; grayscale-filtered photography; status shown only by LED shape and blink (filled = in stock, half = limited, hollow with strike = sold out); stepped, terminal-style motion (typewriter, flicker, count-up, CRT collapse after boot).
**Hero:** the emblem sits inside a rotating radar. A small WebGL scene (Three.js r160, vendored) shows a ring that scans from wireframe to polished chrome; every one of the 23 objects is a blip that pings when the sweep passes (hover for its name and price, click to inspect). If WebGL is missing, the CSS emblem and radar remain.
**Key interactions:** command palette on `/` or Ctrl/Cmd+K (type to filter objects, jump to sections, set filter/sort, open bag; arrows, Enter, Esc); INSPECT drawer with numbered photo frames, key-cap option pickers, specification table, SIGNAL filtered/raw toggle, a big Add to bag key (`/cart/{variantId}:1`) and `[ ]` to step through objects; compare up to 3 objects; card key-caps pre-select the size; crosshair and X/Y coordinates follow the pointer over photos, with a scanline wipe to the second photo on hover; delivery-route lookup (29 zones, Nigeria included); live Regina and Lagos clocks; deep links such as `#mc-015`.
**Kept from the draft:** product data, filter and sort, sold-out handling, newsletter POST to the store (same hidden fields), FAQ, Instagram and mailto links, prices via Intl in CAD with a visible CAD note. No Fit Studio.
**Fonts and files:** fonts are self-hosted in `assets/fonts` (no external requests). Everything is static: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `main.js`, `hero3d.js`, `data/products.js`, `config.js`.
**Known limits:** product photos are shown in grayscale by design (the inspect drawer has a RAW toggle for true colour). The 3D scene is a stylised ring, not a model of a product. The command palette and compare are keyboard-first; on touch they are reached through the CMD key and the compare tray. Scroll-linked reveals need a browser with scroll-driven animations and otherwise simply show the content.
