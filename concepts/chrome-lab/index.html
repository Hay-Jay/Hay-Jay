      05 / HELP

      QUESTIONS. ANSWERED.

      STILL CURIOUS?

      Ask about sizing, custom pieces or a collab and we will get back to you.

      Email us @monochrome.ca
      Where do I buy?

      Every piece is sold through the Monochrome store. Open any product on this page, pick your size and check out there.

      Do you ship to Nigeria and the rest of the world?

      Yes. The store ships from Canada to 29 destinations, including Nigeria, Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom. The full list is in the Worldwide section above. Delivery cost and time are calculated at checkout from your address.

      Which currency are prices in?

      Every price on this site is in Canadian dollars (CAD), the same as the store. If your card is in another currency, your bank handles the conversion.

      How does sizing work?

      Fits are true to size with a relaxed cut on tops. If you are between sizes, go up. Each product page lists its measurements; message us if you want a recommendation.

      Shipping and delivery

      Orders ship from the store after checkout. Delivery time and cost are calculated at checkout based on your address. You will get a tracking update by email once your order leaves.

      Returns and exchanges

      If something does not fit, contact us soon after your order arrives with your order number. Items should be unworn and in original condition. Final terms are shown in the store policies.

      Are drops limited?

      Yes. Pieces are made in small runs and some do not return. Join the drop alerts below to hear about new releases first.

      Custom orders and collaborations

      Interested in custom pieces, a collab or a bulk order? Email us with the details and we will get back to you.
