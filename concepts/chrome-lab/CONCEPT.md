# CHROME LAB — concept notes

**Idea.** The site is a liquid-metal showroom you travel through. One fixed Three.js canvas never unmounts: a mercury blob (hero) buds into orbiting droplets, they gather into the emblem ring and star, and the camera dives through that ring into a chrome tunnel of product-photo planes, then exits to a re-pooled blob behind frosted glass. Scroll position drives everything, so scrolling up plays it in reverse.
**What makes it distinct.** The product showcase is rendered in the scene, not in the DOM: curved photo planes (CDN textures, width-resized) on a weaving path with a mirror floor, chrome hoops and a light at the tunnel end. Photos are monochrome until you point at them. Scroll velocity bends and ripples the planes, splits their RGB, widens the FOV and agitates the blob. Type is huge tight chrome-gradient Archivo (variable width) with a JetBrains Mono instrument layer: vertical HUD with chapter, depth in metres, scroll velocity and a live Regina clock.
**Key interactions.** Planes tilt toward the pointer, grow and turn colour on hover, and open the product view on click or tap (cursor swells to an OPEN disc). The caption card swaps sides to balance each piece; a numbered rail jumps to any piece; the filterable and sortable archive grid, product view (gallery, option chips, sold-out state, `/cart/<variant>:1` link), worldwide destinations list from the store data, FAQ, newsletter, contact links and footer all work as before. Prices are `CA$` via Intl with CAD notes.
**Fallbacks and a11y.** No WebGL, a failed module, or `prefers-reduced-motion` gives a plain DOM card grid for the gallery (reduced motion keeps a still hero blob, no glints or grain). With 3D on, a visually hidden list of real links is in tab order; focusing one scrolls the scene to it and shows a visible label, Enter opens the product. Keyboard focus rings, `inert` background and focus trap in the product view, alt text everywhere, no horizontal scroll from 360 to 1920 wide.
**Known limits.** Needs a GPU for the full effect (the pixel ratio self-lowers if frames are slow); the gallery shows 17 of the 23 products (all other pieces, rings beyond three and the sold-out necklace stay in the archive grid); fonts are self-hosted latin subsets; the 3D module needs an http(s) origin (it is skipped on `file://`, which falls back to the DOM grid); `assets/emblem-a.webp` and `wordmark-a.webp` are alpha versions of the originals made for this concept.
