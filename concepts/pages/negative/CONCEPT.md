# NEGATIVE (multi-page) — MONOCHROME® as a magazine, one spread per URL
**Idea:** the store is Issue 01 of a black-and-white fashion zine and every page is a different kind of spread with folios, running heads and a numeral bleeding off the edge. Photographs are always printed B&W (grayscale plus contrast); colour exists only through the exposure lens. Bodoni Moda + DM Mono, paper and ink spreads, an Invert toggle that swaps them everywhere.
**Page map:** `/` cover, `/shop/` the index, `/products/<handle>/` 23 plates, `/lookbook/` film strip (signature), `/about/` the formula, `/shipping/` distribution, `/faq/` letters, `/contact/` colophon, plus `404.html`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`; all generated by `scripts/build.mjs` from `src/` and `data/products.js`.
**Distinct per page:** cover = giant masthead over a cover photo with an "in this issue" list; index = huge type rows with a cursor-following plate (tap-to-unfold on touch) or a contact sheet of frames with grease-pencil crosses on sold-out pieces; plate = sticky photograph with gallery strip, caption block and printed size labels as option chips; lookbook = pinned horizontal strip of 12 frames (swipe strip on touch and reduced motion); about = drop cap, two-column essay, pull quote and marginalia; shipping = a printed table of 29 countries with Nigeria and Canada reversed out and returns as marginalia; FAQ = Q&A interview format; contact = colophon list and a cut-out subscribe coupon.
**Interactions:** exposure lens (hover or press and hold, plus a Develop colour button), `/` or Ctrl+K command bar that jumps to any page or piece on every page, filter/sort/search/view synced to the URL (`/shop/?c=top`), variant chips set price, stock and the `cart/<variantId>:1` link, keyboard/arrow/touch lookbook, print-like wipes between pages via cross-document View Transitions, instant prefetch on hover/focus/touch.
**Works everywhere:** relative links via a per-page root prefix and `MC.url()`; `--preview` build uses explicit `index.html` links and runs from `file://`; 404.html pins its own base (`--base-path`); no-JS shows the full index, product pages, FAQ and shipping table.
**Known limits:** the pinned lookbook runs only on wide fine-pointer screens (native swipe strip elsewhere); the Custom Jersey text cannot be passed through a cart link; `color-mix()`, `svh` and `@view-transition` need a recent browser (older ones just navigate normally); the 404 page needs JavaScript to find its base on deep missing URLs; sold-out and stock state is a snapshot of `data/products.js`, so rebuild after `sync-products`; Bodoni reads luxury-editorial, so the photography and grain carry the streetwear half.
