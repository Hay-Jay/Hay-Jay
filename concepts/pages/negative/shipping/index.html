    Returns & exchanges

    1. 1

      If something does not fit, contact us soon after your order arrives and include your order number.

    2. 2

      Items should be unworn and in original condition.

    3. 3

      Need another size? Tell us which piece and which size, and we will point you to the right next step.

    4. 4

      The final terms are the store’s. Read the refund policy and the shipping policy before you order.

    How an order travels

    1. 01Choose

      Open a plate, pick your size and use Add to bag. Your bag lives on the store.

    2. 02Check out

      Enter your address. The store shows the shipping rate for your country before you pay. Canada: free standard shipping.

    3. 03Delivered

      You get an email update once your order ships. Rates, not promises: we do not quote delivery times here.

    Fit notes

    Size guidance.

    Tops & jerseys
    Cut relaxed and true to size. If you are between sizes, go up.
    Bottoms
    Cargo shorts and the cargo skirt are listed in the sizes shown on each plate. Not sure? Message us before you order.
    Rings
    Sold by ring size. Pick from the sizes on the plate; between two sizes, go up for comfort.
    Chains & necklaces
    One size. Photographs show how they sit when worn.
    Custom jersey
    Up to 10 characters. Contact us with the details before you order.
    Gift cards
    No size. Choose an amount on the plate.

    Want a recommendation? Email info.mccanada@gmail.com with the piece and the size you usually wear.
