MONOCHROME® is clothing and objects made from three finishes: black, white and chrome. There is no colour of the month and no seasonal palette. Keeping the list that short means every piece you buy works with every piece you already own.

Black is the base. It carries the heavy pieces: the acid-washed hoodie and tees, the cargo shorts and skirt, the jerseys. White is contrast, the 77 jersey and the lettering on the chest. Chrome is the edge: signet rings, star rings, a trinity ring, a chain for your pants, a cross on a chain.

Nothing here needs a matching partner. It is all the same three finishes.

01Small runs

We make pieces in small runs on purpose. When a size sells through it is gone, and some pieces do not come back. Sold-out pieces stay in the index, crossed out in grease pencil, so you can see what you missed instead of wondering.

02Made to mix

Tops are cut relaxed and run true to size; go up if you are between sizes. Layer them, tuck them, chain them. Rings come in sizes. Hardware, objects and details are there to catch the light, not to match anything.

03Two tones, one lens

Every photograph on this site is printed in black and white, because that is the brand. Hold your cursor over a photograph (or press and hold on a phone) and a lens exposes the colour it was shot in. It is the only colour on the site.
