    Monochrome® — Letters — Questions, answered

    Letters & help — p.06

    Questions,answered.

    11 questions we get most, in the order we get them. Each answer is as short as it can be.

    Q.01Where do I buy?
    A.

    Every piece is sold through the Monochrome store. Open any piece in the index, pick your size and use Add to bag. Checkout happens on the store.

    Q.02Do you ship to Nigeria?
    A.

    Yes. Nigeria is on the delivery list, together with 28 other countries (the full list is on the Shipping page). The rate for your address is calculated at checkout.

    Q.03How much is shipping and how long does it take?
    A.

    Shipping is calculated at checkout from your address, so you see the rate for your country before you pay. Canada gets free standard shipping. You will get an email update once your order ships.

    Q.04How does sizing work?
    A.

    Tops have a relaxed cut and run true to size. If you are between sizes, go up. Every piece lists the sizes it comes in, and rings are sold by ring size. Message us if you want a recommendation. There is more on the Shipping page.

    Q.05Which currency are prices in?
    A.

    All prices on this site are in Canadian dollars (CAD), the store’s base currency. Your card issuer converts at checkout if your card is in another currency.

    Q.06Returns and exchanges
    A.

    If something does not fit, contact us soon after your order arrives with your order number. Items should be unworn and in original condition. The final terms are in the store’s refund policy.

    Q.07Are drops limited?
    A.

    Yes. Pieces are made in small runs and some do not return. Sold-out pieces stay in the index, crossed out, so you can see what you missed. Subscribe on the Colophon page to hear about the next issue first.

    Q.08How do gift cards work?
    A.

    The gift card comes in four amounts ($10.00, $25.00, $50.00, $100.00 CAD). Choose one on the gift card page; the recipient chooses their own piece.

    Q.09Can I order a custom jersey?
    A.

    The Custom Monochrome Jersey takes up to 10 characters. Please contact us with your details before you order so we can answer any follow-up questions.

    Q.10A piece I want is sold out. Will it come back?
    A.

    Some pieces return and some do not. We cannot promise either way. The quickest way to find out is to subscribe to drop alerts or to message us on Instagram.

    Q.11Custom orders, collaborations and bulk orders
    A.

    Interested in custom pieces, a collab or a bulk order? Email info.mccanada@gmail.com with the details and we will get back to you.
