    §1Situation

    MONOCHROME® is clothing and objects made from three finishes: black, white and chrome. There is no colour of the month and no seasonal palette. Keeping the list that short means every piece you buy works with every piece you already own.

    Black is the base. It carries the heavy pieces: the acid-washed hoodie and tees, the cargo shorts and skirt, the jerseys. White is contrast, the 77 jersey and the lettering on the chest. Chrome is the edge: signet rings, star rings, a trinity ring, a chain for your pants, a cross on a chain.

    Nothing here needs a matching partner. It is all the same three finishes.

    §2 — Mission objectives

    Four objectives

    1. 01
      Wear black, white and chrome.

      That is the whole palette. Everything on file is one of the three, or a mix of them, and it all works together.

    2. 02
      Keep every run small.

      We make pieces in small runs on purpose. When a size sells through it is gone, and some pieces do not come back. Sold-out assets stay in the register, marked out of stock, so you can see what you missed instead of wondering.

    3. 03
      Build everything to mix.

      Tops are cut relaxed and run true to size; go up if you are between sizes. Layer them, tuck them, chain them. Rings come in sizes. Hardware, objects and details are there to catch the light, not to match anything.

    4. 04
      Deliver.

      The store’s address is Regina, Saskatchewan, Canada, and it delivers to 29 countries, Nigeria and Canada among them. The full list is on the Extraction routes page. Prices are in Canadian dollars.

    §3 — Rules of engagement

    Rules of engagement

    1. Rule 01No colour of the month.

      No seasonal palette, no trend colour. Black, white and chrome, every drop.

    2. Rule 02Between sizes? Go up.

      Tops have a relaxed cut and run true to size. Not sure? Message us before you order.

    3. Rule 03Sold out means sold out.

      Some pieces return and some do not. We will not pretend either way.

    4. Rule 04Nothing needs a matching partner.

      Layer it, tuck it, chain it. It is all the same three finishes.

    5. Rule 05Photographs are filed in black and white.

      That is the brand. Hold your cursor over a photograph (or press and hold on a phone) to declassify the colour it was shot in. It is the only colour on the site.

    6. Rule 06Never explain the outfit.

      Wear the unknown.
