    1. Phase 01Choose

      Open an asset file, pick your size and use Add to bag. Your bag lives on the store.

    2. Phase 02Check out

      Enter your address. The store shows the shipping rate for your country before you pay. Canada: free standard shipping.

    Field-fit notes

    Size guidance

    Tops & jerseys
    Cut relaxed and true to size. If you are between sizes, go up.
    Bottoms
    Cargo shorts and the cargo skirt are listed in the sizes shown on each file. Not sure? Message us before you order.
    Rings
    Sold by ring size. Pick from the sizes on the file; between two sizes, go up for comfort.
    Chains & necklaces
    One size. Photographs show how they sit when worn.
    Custom jersey
    Up to 10 characters. Contact us with the details before you order.
    Gift cards
    No size. Choose an amount on the file.

    Want a recommendation? Email info.mccanada@gmail.com with the piece and the size you usually wear.
