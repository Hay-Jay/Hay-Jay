# CHROME LAB, multi-page: concept notes

**Idea.** The liquid-chrome showroom, split into real pages. The identity is unchanged (Archivo + JetBrains Mono, chrome-gradient type, dark glass with chrome hairlines, HUD rail, one mercury blob and one ring + star), but nothing is a scroll chapter any more: every page has its own URL, and each page loads only the WebGL it needs. No context lives across navigations; fast cross-document View Transitions and hover prefetch cover the hop.
**Page map.** `/` home, `/shop/` (all 23, filter + sort), `/gallery/` (the 3D tunnel), `/products/<handle>/` x 23 (same path as Shopify), `/about/`, `/shipping/`, `/faq/`, `/contact/`, `/404.html`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`. One generator (`scripts/build.mjs`, Node 22, no packages) renders everything from shared partials; the output is committed next to `src/`.
**Distinct per page.** Home is the mercury blob that buds into droplets and re-forms into the emblem as you scroll, with featured pieces as floating glass cards. Gallery is the scene itself: curved photo planes on a weaving path, mirror floor, caption card and numbered rail. Shop is a chrome-hairline grid. Product pages pair a big DOM photo stage with a glass buy panel (sticky bar on phones) and a small live ring + star accent. About is a black + white + chrome equation; Shipping has a build-time SVG dot-globe with an arc from Regina to Nigeria, Canada and Nigeria called out, and every country listed by name; FAQ has live search over native `<details>`; Contact has the signal form.
**One 3D file, four modes.** `js/lab3d.js` runs `hero`, `tunnel`, `ambient` or `off`. Ambient is a dim ring + star drifting behind editorial pages (no blob on phones); on product pages it renders only into a small box.
**Interactions.** Tunnel settles on the nearest piece, clicking a plane opens its real product page, Tab through a hidden list of real links moves the scene. Option chips pick the variant and point Add to bag at `/cart/<variantId>:1` (all 93 available variants verified); sold-out products show Sold out and have no buy link. Filters and sort sync to `?c=` and `?s=` with back/forward. The command bar (`/` or Ctrl/Cmd+K) jumps to any page, category or piece from every page. The clicked product photo travels to its page.
**Resilience.** Shop, product pages, FAQ and the rest read completely without JavaScript (a plain "Buy on the store" link). No WebGL, a lost context or reduced motion gives the DOM grid on `/gallery/` and a still hero. Links are relative with a per-page root prefix and `MC.url()`, so the site works at any base path; `--preview` builds explicit `index.html` links.
**Performance.** Pixel ratio is capped and self-lowers on slow frames, rendering pauses when hidden or off screen and stops as a page is left, product images are lazy and requested with Shopify's `width`.
**Known limits.** Only software WebGL was available for testing, so frame rate on mid-range phones needs a real-device pass. `404.html` is served at arbitrary URLs, so it takes its base from `basePath` in `config.js`. The gallery shows 17 of the 23 pieces. Returns and size copy is deliberately generic (no measurements, no delivery times, only Canada's free standard shipping is named). The 3D scene needs http(s), so on `file://` the gallery shows its grid. Fonts are latin subsets; the raster wordmark is small, so the giant footer mark is live type.
