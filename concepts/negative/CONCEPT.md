# NEGATIVE — MONOCHROME® as a printed fashion zine ("Issue 01 / The Unknown")
**Idea:** the whole store is an issue of a magazine. Every product photo is shown in high-contrast black and white (CSS grayscale + contrast), so the brand name becomes literal; colour only exists through the "exposure" lens.
**Distinct:** spreads alternate paper and ink (a header that follows the page tone, plus an Invert toggle that swaps them like a photographic negative); a masthead and numerals that bleed off the edges; rotated captions, ticker rules, running folios, stable plate numbers; Bodoni Moda (display) + DM Mono (captions), both self-hosted. Chrome is only the emblem and hairline highlights.
**Interactions:** cursor lens reveals the true colour of any photo (press and hold on touch); product index is a huge-type table of contents where hovering a title makes a lagging plate follow the cursor and blur in (tap a row to unfold the plate on touch); contact-sheet view with white-bordered frames and grease-pencil marks for sold out and new; a scroll-driven horizontal lookbook of 12 real photos; counters, clip-path reveals, "Develop colour" toggle on the product spread.
**Kept working:** data/products.js, category filter + sort, product view (gallery, size/colour chips, sold-out state, Add to bag to `${storeUrl}/cart/${variantId}:1`), newsletter POST to `${storeUrl}/contact#contact_form`, FAQ, Instagram and mail links, CAD prices with a visible note. No 3D, no build step, no npm.
**Known limits:** the delivery-country count and ticker come from `DATA.store.shipsTo` in the product snapshot, so they follow the store config; the Custom Jersey text option cannot be passed through a cart link; the pinned horizontal lookbook only runs on wide fine-pointer screens (it is a native swipe strip elsewhere and with reduced motion); `color-mix()` and `svh` need a 2023+ browser; the pointer lens is not shown for keyboard users (the Develop colour button on each product is).
