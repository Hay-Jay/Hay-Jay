# NEGATIVE — MONOCHROME® as a printed fashion zine ("Issue 01 / The Unknown")
**Idea:** the whole store is an issue of a magazine. Every product photo is shown in high-contrast black and white (CSS grayscale + contrast), so the brand name becomes literal; colour only exists through the "exposure" lens.
**Distinct:** spreads alternate paper and ink (a header that follows the page tone, plus an Invert toggle that swaps them like a photographic negative); a masthead and numerals that bleed off the edges; rotated captions, ticker rules, running folios, stable plate numbers; Bodoni Moda (display) + DM Mono (captions), both self-hosted, with the display optical size pinned (opsz 22, italics 14) so hairlines survive the print grain. Chrome is only the emblem and hairline highlights.
**Interactions:** cursor lens reveals the true colour of any photo (press and hold on touch); desktop opens on a huge-type table of contents where hovering a title makes a lagging plate float in the space to the right of the title (never on top of it); phones and touch open on the contact sheet (white-bordered frames, hand-drawn grease-pencil X and circle for sold out) with the TOC one tap away (tap a row to unfold its plate, then "Open the piece"); a scroll-driven horizontal lookbook of 12 real photos (strip moves faster than the page, ~4 screens); counters, clip-path reveals, "Develop colour" toggle on the product spread.
**Product spread:** gallery, size/colour chips, sold-out state, "Add to bag" to `${storeUrl}/cart/${variantId}:1`. On phones and tablets the buy bar is sticky at the bottom of the spread so the buy path is always on the first screen.
**Kept working:** data/products.js, category filter (one swipeable row on phones) + sort, newsletter POST to `${storeUrl}/contact#contact_form`, FAQ, Instagram and mail links, CAD prices with a visible note. No 3D, no build step, no npm. With scripting off the cover and editor's note still show a photograph and a link to the store (the lookbook and index need JS).
**Known limits:** the delivery-country count and ticker come from `DATA.store.shipsTo`; the Custom Jersey text option cannot be passed through a cart link; the pinned horizontal lookbook only runs on wide fine-pointer screens (native swipe strip elsewhere and with reduced motion); `color-mix()` and `svh` need a 2023+ browser; the pointer lens is not shown for keyboard users (Develop colour on each product is); the lead photograph for the Sweatshirt is overridden in `HERO` in main.js (its first frame is half black fabric); the no-JS fallback image URL is hard-coded to one Shopify CDN file; Bodoni reads luxury-editorial, so the photography and grain carry the streetwear half.
