    Monochrome® — Questions, answered

    Letters & help

    Questions,answered.

    Q.01Where do I buy?

    Every piece is sold through the Monochrome store. Open any piece on this page, pick your size and use Add to bag. Checkout happens on the store.

    Q.02How does sizing work?

    Fits are true to size with a relaxed cut on tops. If you are between sizes, go up. Each product page lists its measurements; message us if you want a recommendation.

    Q.03Shipping and delivery

    Orders ship from the store after checkout. Delivery time and cost are calculated at checkout based on your address, and the store lists the countries it delivers to (Nigeria included). You will get a tracking update by email once your order leaves.

    Q.04Which currency are prices in?

    All prices on this page are in Canadian dollars (CAD), the store’s base currency. Your card issuer converts at checkout if your card is in another currency.

    Q.05Returns and exchanges

    If something does not fit, contact us soon after your order arrives with your order number. Items should be unworn and in original condition. Final terms are shown in the store policies.

    Q.06Are drops limited?

    Yes. Pieces are made in small runs and some do not return. Join the drop alerts below to hear about the next issue first.

    Q.07Custom orders and collaborations

    Interested in custom pieces, a collab or a bulk order? Email us with the details and we will get back to you.
